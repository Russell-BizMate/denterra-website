How much does a remote dental insurance verification specialist actually cost?

Blog · October 21, 2026 · 5 min read

Short answer: a full-time remote dental insurance verification specialist from Denterra costs $15 per hour, all-in. At 40 hours a week that is $600 a week, or about $31,200 a year per seat. There are no placement fees, no setup fees, and no long-term contract.

The longer answer is that "cost" depends on what you count. Most practices compare the hourly rate and stop there. The real number is what a seat costs once you add payroll tax, benefits, PTO, recruiting, training, equipment, and the turnover you pay for every time someone leaves.

That is roughly a 2x difference per seat, or about $38,500 a year. In higher-cost markets (California, New York, Massachusetts, major metros) the gap is wider because local wages run higher.

What is included in the $15/hour

  • The team member's pay, paid on time by Denterra
  • Recruiting, screening, and skill verification (HIPAA, dental terminology, top procedure codes, Dentrix / Eaglesoft / Open Dental, major carrier portals)
  • Equipment and a secure workstation setup
  • Payroll and tax handling on Denterra's side
  • Ongoing performance management, coaching, and quarterly KPI reviews
  • A no-cost re-match if the fit isn't right

How billing works

You pay for the first two weeks up front when you get started. After that, every two weeks Denterra runs a standard ACH charge for the previous two weeks your team member worked. No placement fee, no annual contract. If you plan to build a team of more than three people, special pricing is available on the discovery call.

Why not just hire a cheaper virtual assistant?

You can find general virtual assistants for less than $15 an hour. The problem is that insurance verification is not general admin work. A generic VA doesn't know what a frequency limitation is, how to read a breakdown of benefits, or why a missing tooth clause matters for a D6010. Practices that try the generic route usually end up re-training from scratch, then re-hiring when the person leaves for the next gig.

Denterra only places people who already have experience in insurance verification, medical billing, or related admin work, and who want a full-time, long-term role rather than freelance work. That is what keeps the seat filled.

The cost that doesn't show up on the invoice: turnover

Every time a front desk or verification seat turns over, a practice eats weeks of lost production, the owner's or office manager's time, and the cost of claims and verifications that didn't get done in between. Denterra team members earn 2 to 3 times local pay in their home country and have a clear career path (team member, senior, team lead), so leaving stops being the default. For most practices that retention is worth more than the hourly savings.

Bottom line

A remote dental insurance verification specialist through Denterra costs $15 an hour, about $31,200 a year, with everything included. The equivalent in-office seat runs close to $69,700 once you count the full picture.
