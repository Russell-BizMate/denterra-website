Quick answers

Common questions about Denterra.

What is Denterra?
Denterra is a remote dental admin staffing company. We recruit, skill-verify, and manage full-time team members abroad who handle insurance verification, claims and collections, treatment coordination, and credentialing for U.S. dental practices. It was co-founded by a practicing dentist inside Open Door Family Dentistry.
How much does a Denterra team member cost?
$15 per hour, all-in, for a full-time team member working 40 hours a week. That works out to $600 a week or about $31,200 a year per seat, with no placement fees, setup fees, or long-term contract. See the pricing page for the full cost comparison.
What tasks can a remote dental admin handle?
Insurance verification and eligibility, claims submission and A/R follow-up, appeals, pre-authorizations, treatment plan follow-ups, recall and reactivation calls, appointment confirmations, patient payment plans, and provider credentialing (CAQH and carrier applications).
Where are Denterra team members located?
Most team members are in the Philippines, with additional recruiting in South America and Africa, including Zimbabwe and Argentina. All have prior dental or medical admin experience and strong English.
How quickly can someone start?
Most practices have a team member seated and trained within 2 to 3 weeks of the discovery call. Urgent seats can be filled in 7 to 10 days.
Is Denterra HIPAA compliant?
Yes. Every team member completes HIPAA training and certification before placement, works on secure workstation tooling with role-based access, and a BAA is signed with every practice before a team member starts.
Which dental software do Denterra team members know?
Every team member is introduced to Dentrix, Eaglesoft, and Open Dental, usually with deeper experience in at least one, and works across the major U.S. dental insurance carrier portals.
